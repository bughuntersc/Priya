Priya

🌸 In the softest corner of every dawn,
there is a light that speaks your name —
Priya, a melody the heart has known
long before the stars were formed.

You are the quiet in the rushing wind,
the gentle hush of falling snow,
the warmth that lingers in a cup of tea,
the kindness that the world needs so.

✨ May every path you walk be lined with grace,
may joy find you in the smallest things —
for you are beautiful, not just in form,
but in the peace your presence brings.

always, with love
